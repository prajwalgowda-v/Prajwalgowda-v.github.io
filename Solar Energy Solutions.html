Overview of On-Grid Solar Systems

Understanding grid-tied solar technology

What is an On-Grid Solar System?

An on-grid solar system (grid-tied) connects directly to the utility grid through a bi-directional meter (net meter). Most Indian homes with a reliable grid connection choose on-grid solar since this system is powerful enough to support all household energy requirements.

Government Subsidy Benefits

One of the best things about installing on-grid solar at home is the subsidy you get. The Indian government offers homeowners a subsidy for installing a rooftop on-grid solar system ranging between Rs. 30,000 and Rs. 78,000 (depending upon the installed system capacity).

Solar System Components

The building blocks of your solar power system

Solar Panels

Solar panels are the most visible part of the solar system. They're installed on the rooftop, facing the South direction. When sunlight falls on these panels, they absorb that sunlight and convert it into electricity.

Solar Inverter

Without a solar inverter, the entire solar system would have been an impractical model. The solar inverter converts DC into AC, making it usable for household appliances.

Mounting Structures

These are the pillars that support solar panels. They must be of a standard grade to withstand heavy winds during storms and cyclones and hold the weight of the solar panels for 25 years.

Solar Accessories

AC and DC cables, combiner boxes, earthing strips and wires, and MC4 connectors are essential components that make the entire solar grid functional and safe.

Bi-directional Meter

Also known as a net meter, it keeps track of electricity supplied to the grid and electricity imported from the grid, ensuring accurate billing for your solar system.

Longevity

When maintained properly, an on-grid solar system can last for 25 years. Professional maintenance ensures peak efficiency throughout its lifespan.

How It Works

The journey from sunlight to electricity

1

Solar Energy Capture and DC Power Generation

Solar panels, composed of photovoltaic (PV) cells, absorb photons from sunlight and free electrons within the cell's semiconductor material. This movement of electrons generates a direct current (DC).

2

DC-to-AC Conversion via the Solar Inverter

The inverter takes the panel-generated DC and inverts it into alternating current (AC), matching the frequency (50 Hz in India) and voltage of the local grid.

3

Bidirectional Meter Integration

Once converted to grid-compliant AC, the electricity flows through a bi-directional (net) meter. This meter precisely records both incoming units drawn from the grid and outgoing units exported from your solar system.

4

Household Consumption of Solar-Generated AC

During the day, your home's appliances draw AC power directly from the inverter output. Because solar power is typically the first source used, it reduces or altogether replaces the need to import electricity from the grid.

5

Exporting Excess Solar Power

If your panels produce more electricity than your household consumes at any moment, the surplus AC automatically travels back through the bi-directional meter into the grid. Your meter records these exported units, which may earn you financial credit.

6

Importing Grid Power When Needed

After sunset or during low-sunlight periods, the bi-directional meter switches to import mode if your solar output drops below your home's demand. It draws the shortfall from the grid, using up any previously accrued credits first.

Advantages and Disadvantages

Making an informed decision about solar energy

Advantages

  • Energy Production Monitoring: Easy tracking of electricity generated by your solar system
  • Simplified Solar Billing: Easier to understand the solar electricity bill
  • Rapid Payback: Break even in 3-5 years, then enjoy free electricity for 20-22 years
  • Significant Monthly Savings: Substantial reduction in electricity bills
  • No Battery Requirement: Grid acts as the battery, reducing maintenance costs
  • Most Affordable Option: On-grid systems are the most cost-effective of the three types
  • Exclusive Solar Subsidy: Government subsidy only available for on-grid systems

Disadvantages

Power Outages

When there's a power cut from the grid, the on-grid solar system automatically shuts down. This interruption in the supply of power is known as a power outage.

Anti-Islanding Protection

The main reason an on-grid solar inverter is designed to automatically shut down if the grid fails is to eliminate safety risks. This feature protects electrical maintenance personnel from electric shocks.

On-grid vs Off-grid

Hands down, on-grid solar systems in urban areas with a reliable grid are unbeatable for meeting a home's power requirements. You don't have to worry about costs related to battery upkeep, it's more affordable than an off-grid solar system, and the government offers a subsidy on on-grid systems, not off-grid systems.

Installation Process

Professional installation by our expert team

1

Rooftop Inspection and Site Assessment

A trained engineer surveys your rooftop to map shadow-casting objects and determine the optimal panel orientation (usually south in India) and tilt angle (approximately equal to your latitude). This analysis maximizes sunlight exposure and long-term energy yield.

2

Roof Mounting and Panel Clamping

Mounting structures are securely fixed to your roof using corrosion-protected rails and fasteners. Panels are then clamped onto these rails, following the manufacturer's torque specifications to prevent damage.

3

Inverter Installation & Electrical Wiring

Solar strings (series-connected panels) are routed to the DC combiner box, then to the inverter, which converts DC into grid-compatible AC. AC output is channeled through an AC combiner box to your bi-directional meter, with all cables protected in conduit or trays.

4

Earthing & Lightning Protection

Dedicated earthing rods and cables bond both DC and AC components to the ground, safeguarding against electrical faults. A lightning arrester mounted above the panels intercepts strikes and diverts surge energy safely to the earthing grid.

5

System Commissioning & Monitoring Setup

Technicians verify string voltages, phase sequence, and inverter settings before energizing the system. Finally, the inverter is linked to your Wi-Fi for real-time performance monitoring, ensuring any issues are spotted and resolved by Naptico Green Energy Solars's team promptly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about solar energy

Does on-grid solar need a battery?

No. That's the beauty of installing an on-grid solar system. It does not require a battery connection like off-grid and hybrid solar systems, which makes it easier to maintain and the most affordable option of the three types.

Do solar panels work at night?

No. Solar panels generate electricity when photons from sunlight knock electrons loose in the PV cells. At night, an on-grid solar system draws power from the grid to keep a house running.

What is the difference between on-grid and hybrid solar systems?

The main difference is that a hybrid system is connected to the grid and batteries, while an on-grid solar system is connected only to the grid.

What is the on-grid solar panel price in India?

The prices vary based on the type of solar panels. For instance, the latest technology, MonoPERC half-cut solar panels in India, cost Rs. 24 per watt.
