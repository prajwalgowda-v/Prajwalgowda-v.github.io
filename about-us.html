Our Story

From a small startup to India's leading solar energy provider

Our Journey

Naptico Green Energy Solars is a Bengaluru-based company specializing in sustainable energy solutions, with a strong focus on solar technology and services. The company is committed to advancing clean, renewable energy for both residential and commercial customers, aiming to create a greener and more energy-independent future.

Starting with just a small office in Bangalore and a handful of dedicated employees, we focused on providing high-quality solar solutions to residential customers. Our commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction quickly earned us a reputation as a reliable solar provider.

Naptico Green Energy Solars operates out of Bengaluru, Karnataka, India, and was incorporated in August 2025. The company excels in delivering innovative technology and energy solutions, striving to empower businesses and households to transition smoothly to solar power.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission: To accelerate India's transition to sustainable energy by providing innovative, reliable, and affordable solar solutions that empower individuals, businesses, and communities to harness the power of the sun.

Vision: Naptico Green Energy Solars envisions a future where every home and business can harness the power of the sun, leading the way toward energy independence and environmental stewardship.

We envision a future where every home and business in India is powered by clean, renewable energy, reducing carbon emissions and creating a healthier planet for future generations.
