Privacy Policy

At Naptico Green Energy Solars, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

Our Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our solar energy solutions

Information We Collect

At Naptico Green Energy Solars, we collect various types of information to provide you with the best possible service. This information helps us understand your needs and deliver personalized solar energy solutions.

The types of information we may collect include:

  • Personal identification information: Name, email address, phone number, and physical address.
  • Property information: Details about your property, including location, roof characteristics, and energy consumption patterns.
  • Technical information: Information about your current electrical system and energy usage history.
  • Financial information: Payment details and financing information for solar installations.
  • Communication data: Records of our interactions with you, including phone calls, emails, and meeting notes.

How We Collect Your Information

We collect information through various channels when you interact with our company:

  • Direct interactions: When you contact us via phone, email, or in person.
  • Website forms: When you fill out contact forms, quote requests, or other forms on our website.
  • Site visits: When our technicians visit your property to assess your solar installation needs.
  • Automatic collection: Our website may automatically collect certain information through cookies and similar technologies.
  • Third parties: With your consent, we may receive information from credit agencies, government entities, or other service providers.

Note: We only collect information that is necessary for providing our solar energy services and improving our customer experience.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for various purposes related to our business operations and service delivery:

  • Service provision: To design, install, and maintain solar energy systems tailored to your needs.
  • Communication: To respond to your inquiries, provide updates, and send relevant information about our services.
  • Administrative purposes: To process payments, manage warranties, and maintain customer records.
  • Service improvement: To analyze usage patterns and feedback to enhance our products and services.
  • Marketing: With your consent, to inform you about new products, services, and special offers.
  • Legal compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and industry standards.

Information Sharing and Disclosure

We respect your privacy and do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only under specific circumstances:

  • Service providers: With trusted third-party companies who assist us in operating our business, such as installation partners, financing institutions, and payment processors.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, regulation, or government request.
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of company assets.
  • Protection of rights: To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property, or that of our customers or the public.
  • With your consent: When you have given us explicit permission to share your information for specific purposes.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

  • Encryption: We use industry-standard encryption to protect sensitive data during transmission and storage.
  • Access controls: We restrict access to personal information to authorized employees who need it to perform their job functions.
  • Employee training: We provide regular training to our employees on data protection best practices.
  • Security assessments: We conduct periodic security assessments to identify and address potential vulnerabilities.
  • Physical security: We maintain physical security measures at our facilities to protect paper records and electronic systems.

Important: While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: You have the right to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: You can request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: You may request that we delete your personal information, subject to certain legal exceptions.
  • Portability: You can request to receive your personal information in a portable format.
  • Objection: You can object to the processing of your personal information for certain purposes.
  • Withdrawal of consent: If we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@napticogreenenergysolars.com or call us at 080 2952 7671.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience and analyze website traffic:

  • Essential cookies: Necessary for the website to function properly and cannot be disabled.
  • Analytical cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting anonymous information.
  • Functional cookies: Remember your preferences and personalize your experience on our website.
  • Advertising cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertisements and track marketing campaign effectiveness.

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings or by using the cookie consent banner on our website. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so that we can take appropriate action.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, services, or for operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. When we make changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
  • Post the revised policy on our website.
  • Notify you of significant changes through email or other communication channels.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information.
