What is ETC Solar Water Heater?

Advanced technology for efficient water heating

Evacuated Tube Collector (ETC) solar water heaters use advanced vacuum tube technology to capture solar energy with exceptional efficiency. Each tube consists of two glass tubes with a vacuum between them, creating superior insulation properties that minimize heat loss.

Key Components:

  • Evacuated glass tubes
  • Heat pipes with copper fins
  • Insulated storage tank
  • Supporting frame structure

Ideal For:

  • Residential homes
  • Hotels and resorts
  • Hospitals and hostels
  • Industrial processes

Naptico Green Energy Solars offers premium ETC solar water heaters that combine cutting-edge technology with robust construction to deliver reliable hot water while significantly reducing your energy costs.

How ETC Solar Water Heaters Work

The science behind efficient water heating

1

Solar Energy Absorption

Sunlight passes through the outer glass tube and strikes the inner tube's selective coating, which absorbs solar radiation and converts it to heat.

2

Heat Transfer to Heat Pipe

The absorbed heat is transferred to the heat pipe inside the tube, containing a small amount of liquid that vaporizes when heated.

3

Vapor Rises to Condenser

The hot vapor rises to the top of the heat pipe where it comes into contact with the condenser, a copper bulb that transfers heat to the water.

4

Heat Exchange

As the vapor condenses back to liquid, it releases its heat to the water in the storage tank through a heat exchanger.

5

Circulation and Storage

The condensed liquid returns to the bottom of the heat pipe, and the cycle repeats. Heated water rises to the top of the storage tank by natural convection.

Benefits of ETC Solar Water Heaters

Why ETC technology is superior

Energy Efficiency

ETC solar water heaters achieve 60-70% thermal efficiency, significantly higher than flat plate collectors (50-60%) and electric geysers (90-95% but using expensive electricity).

All-Weather Performance

The vacuum insulation minimizes heat loss to the surroundings, allowing ETC heaters to work efficiently even in cold climates and during cloudy days.

Easy Maintenance

Individual tubes can be replaced without shutting down the entire system, making maintenance simple and cost-effective. Broken tubes don't affect the system's overall performance.

Cost Savings

With a lifespan of 15-20 years and minimal maintenance costs, ETC solar water heaters offer excellent ROI, typically paying for themselves within 2-4 years through electricity savings.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an ETC solar water heater last?

ETC solar water heaters typically have a lifespan of 15-20 years. The glass tubes are durable and resistant to weathering, while the storage tank is made of high-grade stainless steel or mild steel with anti-corrosion coating.

What happens if a tube breaks?

One of the advantages of ETC systems is that individual tubes can be replaced without shutting down the entire system. If a tube breaks, the system continues to function with reduced efficiency until the damaged tube is replaced.

Do ETC water heaters work in winter?

Yes, ETC water heaters are designed to work efficiently in winter. The vacuum insulation minimizes heat loss, allowing them to perform well even in cold climates. However, efficiency may be reduced on extremely cloudy days.

What size ETC water heater do I need?

The size depends on your hot water consumption. For a family of 4, a 100-150 liter system is typically sufficient. Our experts can help determine the right size based on your specific needs and usage patterns.
